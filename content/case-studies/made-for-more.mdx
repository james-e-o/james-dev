## The brief

Made for More is a sample development case study for a community organisation that needed a more welcoming, accessible place to tell its story and guide people towards getting involved.

## The process

The experience began with the essential questions: who arrives here, what are they trying to do, and what might get in their way? I paired a small content structure with low-fidelity layouts before implementing the responsive page.

We kept the language direct, made the primary actions easy to find, and checked keyboard and small-screen behaviour as part of the build rather than as a final layer.

## The outcome

A clear, responsive front-end foundation and a content structure the organisation can maintain. Add the real launch link and approved impact measures when available.

## Tools & role

Next.js, TypeScript, accessible HTML and CSS. Role: UX, design and front-end development.
