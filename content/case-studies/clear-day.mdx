## The brief

Clear Day is a sample product-design case study about helping a small, busy team feel more in control of its shared work. The first task was not to design a prettier dashboard—it was to understand where coordination was breaking down.

## The process

I began with short conversations about a typical work week, then mapped the hand-offs and repeated interruptions people described. A lightweight prototype helped the team react to the proposed flow before any interface decisions were treated as final.

The early concept focused on clear ownership, a visible next action and fewer competing signals. Feedback was used to revise the structure and simplify the hand-off.

## The outcome

The sample outcome is a tested prototype and a clearer set of product questions for the next phase. No performance metrics are claimed in this placeholder case study.
